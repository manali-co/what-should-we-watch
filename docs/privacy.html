What Should We Watch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

What Should We Watch (“the app”, “we”, “us”) helps you decide what to watch tonight. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We built the app to need as little personal data as possible: you can use the whole core experience as a guest without an account.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. There is no advertising SDK and no cross-app tracking.

Who we are

The app is operated by Manali Apps (New York, USA). For any privacy question or request, contact us at ayush.agrawal7661@gmail.com.

Information we collect

If you sign in (optional — via Apple, Google, or email through our authentication provider Clerk): your name and email address as provided by that sign-in method. Passwords, if any, are handled by Clerk and never seen by us.

How you use the app, so it can learn what you like:

  • The moods/vibes you pick and the moment context you’re watching in (e.g. time of day, weekend).
  • Your decisions on films — like, maybe, dislike, watched — and any reactions you leave.
  • The streaming services and country/region you tell us you use.
  • A short, model-generated summary of your taste, derived from the above to improve recommendations.
  • Your shortlist of films you’ve saved.

A device identifier. In guest mode we use a random per-device id (not tied to your identity) so your choices and shortlist work on that device. Much guest data also stays on the device itself.

Basic technical logs. To keep the app running and secure, our systems record diagnostic data — such as the requests made, errors, and performance timings — via Microsoft Azure Application Insights. This is operational data used to run and debug the service, not to identify you.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or your device’s advertising identifier. Face ID / Touch ID, if you enable it, is handled entirely by your device’s operating system — we never receive your biometric data.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core function: recommend films that fit your mood and taste.
  • To remember your shortlist, services, and decisions so the app works across sessions.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the service.

Our recommendations are generated with large-language and embedding models. Your mood inputs, a summary of your taste, and film metadata are sent to our AI provider to rank suggestions; we do not use this to identify you, and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with (service providers)

We use a small set of processors that handle data on our behalf under their own security terms:

  • Clerk — sign-in and account management.
  • Microsoft Azure — hosting, database, AI (including Azure OpenAI), and diagnostics/monitoring (Application Insights) in our cloud account.
  • OpenAI — additional model inference used for ranking, via API. Not used to train models on your data.
  • Movie of the Night (Streaming Availability API) — the film catalog and streaming-availability data (this is data we receive, not your personal data).

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the service.

Data retention

We keep account and usage data while your account is active or your device continues using the app. You can delete it at any time (see below), after which we remove it from our live systems, subject to short-lived backups and any retention the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Use as a guest — no account, minimal data.
  • Export your data — the app’s Settings include an export of your data.
  • Delete your account and data — the app’s Settings include account deletion.
  • Depending on where you live (e.g. the EEA/UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access controls, a managed cloud) to protect your data. No method is perfectly secure, but we work to keep it safe.

International transfers

We and our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, with appropriate safeguards where required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we’ll change the “last updated” date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app.

This document is provided for transparency and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed by the operator before publication.
